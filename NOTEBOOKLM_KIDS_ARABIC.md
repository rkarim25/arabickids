# Kids Arabic TV & Hikayat · Complete Showrunner & Story Guide

Welcome to **Kids Arabic TV** and **Hikayat** (حكايات). This guide is the official source documentation for scripting, video production, educational planning, and audio podcasts for young Arabic learners (ages 2–7).

---

## 1. Show Philosophy & Tone: "The Gentle Watch"

- **Tone:** Calm, warm, friendly, natural, and unhurried (inspired by *Mister Rogers* and *Bluey*).
- **Zero Condescension:** No yelling, no loud sound effects, no hyperactive cartoon screaming.
- **Visuals:** Warm 3D Pixar-style animation in sunny, peaceful nature and home environments.
- **Pedagogy:** Arabic first, with gentle English conceptual anchors, focusing on natural word acquisition and clean phonetics with full diacritics (tashkeel).

---

## 2. Main Characters

1. **🐰 Bunny (الْأَرْنَبُ اللَّطِيف):**
   - *Appearance:* Fluffy white rabbit, long upright pink-lined ears, soft blue cotton/denim vest.
   - *Personality:* Gentle, curious, loves carrots, sweet and friendly.
2. **🐵 Monkey (الْقِرْدُ الْمَرِح):**
   - *Appearance:* Playful light-brown monkey with peach face, round ears, curly expressive tail.
   - *Personality:* Cheerful, acrobatic, loves yellow bananas, shares with Bunny.
3. **👦 Adam (أَدَم):**
   - *Appearance:* 5-year-old boy, curly textured dark hair, rosy cheeks, warm teal crewneck sweater, blue trousers, red sneakers.
   - *Personality:* Inquisitive, cheerful, loves exploring the house, reading, and going to the masjid.
4. **👧 Maryam (مَرْيَم):**
   - *Appearance:* 2-year-old toddler, twin hair puffs tied with bright red ribbon bows, pink floral dress.
   - *Personality:* Sweet, giggly, loves clapping and stacking blocks.
5. **🧔 Baba (بَابَا):**
   - *Appearance:* Tall, kind father with a neat dark beard and moustache, clean white thobe collar.
   - *Personality:* Warm, patient, leads the family to the mosque.
6. **🧕 Mama (مَامَا):**
   - *Appearance:* Kind, radiant mother with a draped soft teal headscarf (hijab) and coral collar.
   - *Personality:* Loving, nurturing, cooks delicious meals in the kitchen.
7. **🐱 Lulu (لُولُو):**
   - *Appearance:* Very fluffy, plump ginger tabby cat with emerald green eyes, cream muzzle, and white whiskers.
   - *Personality:* Content, loves sneaking into boxes and sleeping on rugs and cushions.

## 3. The 2-Part Video Structure (30–45s Duration)

Every episode on **Kids Arabic TV** follows a clean 2-part structure:

### Part 1: Arabic Story / Action Clip (12–15s)
- Pure animated storytelling in Google Flow (3D animation).
- Expressive, authentic Arabic narration with nature/ambient sound.
- On-screen diacritized Arabic lettering.

### Part 2: Gentle Teaching Spotlight (15–20s)
- Highlight the 1–2 target words with clean phonetics (broken into syllables).
- A warm, friendly English explanation of the meaning.
- 1 simple decodable sentence combining the target words.
- Warm closing: *«مَعَ السَّلَامَة!»* (See you next time!).

## 5. Website Interactive Hub (Hikayat)

The website companion site (rkarim25.github.io/arabickids) provides the interactive practice:
1. **Interactive Tap Soundboard:** Every Arabic word can be tapped to hear pre-rendered native Arabic pronunciation.
2. **Decodable Storybooks:** 8 illustrated picture books with graduated reading levels (Level 1 to Level 5).
3. **Listening Games & Phonics Stages:** Multi-sensory reinforcement ensuring passive viewers become active, confident readers.
